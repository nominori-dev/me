## Overview

A work-in-progress service desk exploring how support teams can organize requests, ownership and day-to-day operations in one place.

## Approach

The documented roadmap covers ticket handling, user roles, assignments and notifications, alongside longer-term ideas such as service levels and a knowledge base. These are planned features, not claims of completed functionality.

## Technical details

The documented stack includes PostgreSQL, Spring Data JPA, Liquibase, Docker and Testcontainers. The repository also describes a separate showcase environment with a Next.js frontend.

## Access

The repository is currently private and the project is described as educational.
