## The idea

Business platforms often have to bring content, accounts and operational workflows together. This project explores what that shared foundation could look like in a single web application.

## The approach

The repository brings together Next.js, Payload CMS and authentication tooling. The direction is a modular platform where content management and application interfaces can evolve alongside each other.

## In progress

This is an unfinished project. The repository contains an application foundation and dependencies; the full product scope, integrations and outcomes are still to be defined.
